## Who we are

StackBinary is a custom software development and AI engineering company from India. We build software AI-native: systems designed around AI from the first line of code, not legacy products with a chatbot bolted on.

Everything we ship belongs to the client who paid for it: **data, code and IP**. That last part is the whole point.

- **Cloud architecture, migration and full-stack product engineering**
